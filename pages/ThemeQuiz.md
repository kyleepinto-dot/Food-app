# theme.py Quiz

Use [theme.py](theme.py) and answer each question with the correct multiple-choice letter.

After each question there is a blank space for your response.

## Multiple Choice Questions

1. What is the primary purpose of `ThemeColors`?

A. Handle barcode scanning and decoding.

B. Centralize shared UI color and spacing tokens.

C. Manage network retries.

D. Parse AI profile responses.

Your answer: b


2. Which token pair controls button foreground/background contrast for brand actions?

A. `TEXT_PRIMARY` and `TEXT_SECONDARY`

B. `GREEN_TEXT` and `GREEN_SURFACE`

C. `BRAND_PRIMARY` and `BRAND_ON_PRIMARY`

D. `CARD_RADIUS_INNER` and `CARD_PADDING`

Your answer: c


3. What do `CARD_RADIUS_OUTER`, `CARD_RADIUS_INNER`, and `CARD_PADDING` represent?

A. API response limits.

B. Shared geometry/layout tokens for cards.

C. Barcode checksum constants.

D. Font fallback order.

Your answer: b


4. Why define `TEXT_PRIMARY`, `TEXT_SECONDARY`, `TEXT_TERTIARY`, and `TEXT_INACTIVE` separately?

A. To support hierarchy/semantics in text styling across the app.

B. To auto-translate text content.

C. To generate random color palettes per page.

D. To speed up HTTP requests.

Your answer: a


5. What does `DIVIDER = "#DFE3E6"` likely style?

A. Camera lens calibration.

B. Thin separating lines between UI sections.

C. Product image source switching.

D. AI fallback prompts.

Your answer: b


6. `GREEN_SURFACE` and `GREEN_SURFACE_SOFT` are described as what kind of family?

A. Error-state colors.

B. Green-forward surfaces for food facts pages.

C. Bluetooth connection colors.

D. Animated gradient stops.

Your answer: b


7. What is the role of `PAGE_BACKGROUND` versus `SHELL_BACKGROUND` comments?

A. They explain outside-page camera overlays.

B. They distinguish page backdrop from the rounded shell container styling.

C. They define dark mode only.

D. They disable touch controls.

Your answer: b


8. Which token is intended for preview placeholder regions when no image is present?

A. `PROGRESS_TRACK`

B. `PREVIEW_FALLBACK_BACKGROUND`

C. `TRACKER_TEXT`

D. `ACCENT_YELLOW`

Your answer: b
